Questions from parents

Testing sessions

What is a testing session?

A short, 15-minute visit where your child tries the app. We watch what is clear and what is tricky, both in the science and in how the app works, so we can make it better before launch.

Who can take part?

Children in Kindergarten and 1st grade (ages 5–7), with a parent or other grown-up.

Where do sessions take place?

Right now sessions are in person, at a place that works for you. We are planning online sessions too and will add details here when they are ready.

Do I stay with my child?

Yes. A parent or other grown-up stays with the child the whole time.

What do you record?

No video and no audio. We take short written notes about the app, for example which question was confusing. The notes never include your child’s name.

Is anything my child does saved?

No. Your child plays on our iPad, and we keep no progress or any other record of their play.

Does it cost anything?

No. Taking part is free.

How do I sign up?

Leave your email in the sign-up form. We’ll write to you to find a time.

The app

What is Octonia?

A read-aloud science workbook for the iPad. Children answer short questions about the world: they tap, sort, match and predict. When they make a mistake, they hear a short explanation of the idea behind it.

My child can’t read yet. Is that a problem?

No. Every question is read aloud, and each answer has its own listen button.

Which topics does it cover?

We start with Animals. Living Things & Plants, Earth & Sky, and Human Body & Feelings are on the way, with more planned. Our learning design draws on NGSS (the US science standards), Cambridge Primary Science, and Singapore’s Primary Science Syllabus.

Will my child feel pressured?

No. There are no timers, and a child can try again as many times as they need. Mistakes are part of learning.

Are there ads?

No. There are no ads in the app.

Can I see how my child is doing?

Yes. A grown-ups area, behind a simple grown-up question, shows which topics your child has practised and which questions were tricky.

Which devices does it work on?

iPad only for now.

When does it launch, and how much will it cost?

We haven’t set a launch date or price yet. If you leave your email, we’ll tell you when Octonia launches.

Privacy

What happens to my child’s data in the app?

Your child’s progress stays on your iPad. It is not sent to us or anyone else. The app has no analytics and no tracking. See the Privacy Policy for details.

What do you do with my email?

We use it only for testing and launch news. We never sell it or use it for ads. To have it deleted, write to privacy@octonia.app.

Still have a question?

Write to us at hello@octonia.app.
