From Animals

The bat and the butterfly both fly with their wings. Whose wing is really a hand?

A child picks: “Both.” Not quite

“A bat’s wing has long finger bones inside. A butterfly’s wing doesn’t.”

From Animals

The hermit crab grows. What happens next?

A child predicts: “Its shell grows too.” Not quite

Its borrowed shell doesn’t grow. It finds a bigger one.

From Animals

A cricket can hear its friends sing, but it has no ears on its head! Its ears are tiny holes somewhere else. Tap where you think the cricket hears.

A child taps: the front legs. ✓ Right!

“A cricket hears with its front legs! Same job — hearing — but a very different body part.”
