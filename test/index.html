Body Parts · Think Deeper

A bat and a butterfly both have wings. Whose wing is really a hand?

A Possible Mix-Up

“Both — they both have wings.”

Octonia Explains

A bat’s wing has long finger bones inside. A butterfly’s wing doesn’t.

The answer: the bat’s.

Shells · Predict What Happens

The hermit crab grows. What happens next?

A Possible Mix-Up

“Its shell grows too.”

Octonia Explains

The crab grew, but its borrowed shell did not. So the crab moved to a bigger one.

The answer: it finds a bigger shell.
