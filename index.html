Big science, made playful

NGSS-aligned challenges for curious young explorers.

NGSS means Next Generation Science Standards: a research-based science education framework focused on inquiry and real-world skills.

Life Science

Plants, animals, our bodies, habitats, life cycles, traits, food chains, ecosystems.

Earth & Space Science

Weather, seasons, sun, moon, stars, sky patterns, landforms, water, climate.

Physical Science

Materials, motion, pushes and pulls, light, sound, heat, magnets, and change.

Scientific Thinking

Predictions, patterns, cause and effect, observations, and conclusions.

Team

We’re a team of scientists, educators, and parents based in Massachusetts, building a game that helps kids think like scientists—asking questions, observing, and understanding how the world works. It’s based on learning science and designed to be fun and engaging.
