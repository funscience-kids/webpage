Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy.

When you sign up on this website, we collect your email address to add you to our waitlist and notify you about early access to our product.

We will only use your email to contact you when early access becomes available. Only if you choose to participate in our customer interview, we will contact you about it as well.

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties.

Your information may be stored using secure third-party services (such as website hosting or email tools) only to operate this website and communicate with you.

We retain your email address until we contact you about early access or until you request deletion. Our first email about product launch will include an option to unsubscribe from future messages. If you would like your data removed, you can contact us at: funscienceapp [at] gmail [dot] com.

Facebook Pixel on our Website

We use the 'visitor action pixels' from Facebook Inc. on our Website. This allows user behavior to be tracked after they have been redirected to our Website by clicking on a Facebook ad. This enables us to measure the effectiveness of Facebook ads for market research purposes. The data collected in this way is anonymous to us and we do not see individual users' personal data. This data is stored and processed by Facebook in accordance with Facebook's Data Usage Policy.
