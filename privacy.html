Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

The short version

  • In the app: your child’s progress stays on your iPad. No ads, no accounts, no analytics, no tracking.
  • On this website: we keep only the email a grown-up gives us to sign up for testing. No cookies, no trackers.
  • Testing sessions: we ask a parent first, record no video or audio, and keep no record of your child’s play.
  • Questions or deletion: write to privacy@octonia.app.

This policy explains what information Octonia (“we”, “us”) handles in the Octonia Science Workbook app (“the app”) and on this website, octonia.app. Octonia is made for children, and this website is for their parents and other grown-ups.

1. The app

What stays on the device

To work, the app keeps a small amount of information on the device where it is installed:

  • each child profile’s first name or nickname and the emoji avatar the child picks (up to four profiles);
  • which questions were answered, how many tries they took, and when — so the app can show stars, bring back tricky questions, and fill the grown-ups area;
  • app settings, such as whether sound is muted.

This information is stored only on that device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it, and we cannot recover it for you.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, logins or passwords.
  • No ads.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No use of the camera, microphone or location.
  • No chat, sharing or links to social media for children.

Read-aloud voice

Questions are read aloud with voice recordings that ship inside the app, or, when a recording is not available, with the text-to-speech voice built into the device. The app never records your child’s voice.

How to delete your child’s data

  • One child: open the grown-ups area from the home screen, answer the grown-up question, go to Manage kids, and tap Delete next to the child. This erases that child’s profile and progress from the device.
  • Everything: delete the app from the device. All of its data is removed with it.

2. This website

Your email, for testing sign-ups

If you sign up for testing, we receive the email address you type in and the time you sent it. We use it to contact you to arrange a testing session with your child, to invite you to the beta, and to tell you when Octonia launches. We do not sell it, rent it, or use it for advertising. The form asks for nothing else — no names and no details about your child.

The form sends your email to a Google Apps Script form handler, a Google service that saves it in a Google Sheet in our team’s Google account. Only our team can open that sheet. Emails you send to our @octonia.app addresses are forwarded to the same Google account.

We keep your email until you ask us to delete it, and no longer than 12 months after Octonia launches.

No cookies, no trackers

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising pixels or tracking scripts. Its fonts and images are served from octonia.app itself, not from third parties.

Our hosting provider, Vercel, may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver the site and keep it secure. We do not use them to identify or track visitors.

3. Testing sessions and the beta

Testing sessions

Testing sessions usually take place in person, with a parent or other grown-up present. Before a session, we tell you what will happen and how long it takes, and we ask for your permission. We do not record video or audio. We only take written notes about how the app worked — for example, which question was confusing — and the notes do not include your child’s name. We do not keep your child’s progress or any other record of your child’s play from the session.

The beta (Apple TestFlight)

We plan to share the beta through Apple’s TestFlight. With TestFlight, Apple automatically shares crash logs and basic usage data with us — such as how many sessions and crashes there were, the day the beta was installed, and the version — and, if you were invited by email, your name and email address. If you send feedback through TestFlight, Apple shares it with us too; it can include a screenshot, your comment and technical details about the device. Apple handles TestFlight data under its own TestFlight privacy notice. We use this only to fix problems in the beta and do not share it with anyone else. The app itself works the same in the beta: your child’s progress stays on the device.

4. Children’s privacy

The app does not collect personal information from children: everything a child does in it stays on the device. This website is meant for adults, and we do not knowingly collect information from children here. If you believe a child has sent us their email, write to us and we will delete it.

We plan to improve our questions using statistics about how they work — for example, how often a question is answered correctly on the first try. If the app ever starts sending such statistics, or adds syncing between devices, we will update this policy first, describe exactly what is sent, and ask for a parent’s permission where the law requires it. Until then, nothing about your child leaves the device.

5. Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct, or delete the email you gave us. Write to privacy@octonia.app, and we will reply within 30 days.

6. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
